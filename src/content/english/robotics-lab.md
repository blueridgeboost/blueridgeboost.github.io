<h3 class="lab-card-title">Adult Coach &amp; Child Supervision</h3>
<p><strong>Basic membership does not include BRB-provided child supervision or childcare — basic membership is not a drop-off program.</strong></p>
<p>Every student or team on basic membership must have a designated adult coach present throughout the session. That adult is responsible for supervising the children, managing behavior, and ensuring they follow lab rules.</p>
<p>BRB employees assist with equipment and enforce equipment-use requirements. They are not the children's supervising adults under basic membership.</p>
<p>Students may not remain in the lab without their designated adult coach. If the coach leaves, the students must leave with them unless a replacement supervising adult has been arranged and confirmed.</p>
<p><strong>Private and one-on-one BRB coaching sessions include supervision</strong> by the booked BRB coach during the confirmed coaching time. Supervision ends when the booked session ends; outside those windows, basic-membership rules apply.</p>

<h3 class="lab-card-title">Equipment Care</h3>
<p>Equipment damaged through normal, responsible use will be repaired or replaced at no additional charge. Damage caused by misuse or abuse may result in a repair or replacement charge. Please report equipment problems promptly.</p>

<h3 class="lab-card-title">Safe Equipment Use</h3>
