<h3>Policy Overview</h3>
<p>Blue Ridge Boost maintains a <strong>no-refund policy</strong> for all sales. Transfers to similar activities may be available upon request.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>General Policy: No Refunds</h2>
<p>We maintain a strict no-refund policy for all sales. This policy applies to, but is not limited to, the following situations:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>Customer-initiated enrollment cancellations</li>
<li>Missed events due to student absence</li>
<li>Program or instructor dissatisfaction</li>
<li>Dismissal resulting from conduct violations</li>
</ul>
<p>While we do not offer refunds, we value your feedback. We are committed to continuously improving our programs to meet your expectations. If you have concerns or suggestions, please don't hesitate to share them with us.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Transfers</h2>
<p>While we do not offer refunds, we understand that circumstances may change. If you need to modify your child's enrollment, you may request a transfer to a similar activity, subject to the following conditions:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>Transfers must be requested in writing at least <strong>10 business days</strong> prior to the start of the original activity</li>
<li>Transfers are subject to availability in the desired activity</li>
<li>The activity to which you are transferring must be of equal or lesser value than the original activity</li>
<li>If the new activity is of lesser value, no refund will be issued for the price difference</li>
<li>If the new activity is of greater value, you will be responsible for paying the difference in price</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Subscription Cancellation</h2>
<p>Subscriptions may be cancelled by the customer. No refunds are given for the charges already made.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Subscription Transfers</h2>
<p>If your child is enrolled in a subscription and you wish to transfer to a different activity, the following additional conditions apply:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>The transfer will take effect at the start of the next billing cycle</li>
<li>No prorated refunds or credits will be issued for the current billing cycle</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Cancelled Events</h2>
<p>In the event that Blue Ridge Boost LLC cancels an event due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., instructor illness, inclement weather, facility issues), we will make every effort to reschedule the event. If rescheduling is not possible, a refund will be issued for the value of the cancelled event.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section policy-highlight-warning">
<h2>Exceptions</h2>
<p><strong>Exceptions to this policy may be made at the sole discretion of Blue Ridge Boost LLC. Such exceptions will be determined on a case-by-case basis and will require approval from a company director.</strong></p>
</div>

<div class="policy-notice">
<p>By enrolling your child in activities provided by Blue Ridge Boost LLC, you acknowledge and agree to this no refund policy. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our office prior to enrolling.</p>
</div>
