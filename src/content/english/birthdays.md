<div class="birthday-faq">
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>Can we bring our own food or cake?</summary><p>Yes — you're welcome to bring your own cake, food, and drinks with any package. Please let us know about any allergies in your guest list so we can accommodate.</p></details>
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>How early can we arrive to set up?</summary><p>Setup access is included in your booked time slot. If you need extra setup time, mention it when you request the party and we'll see what we can do.</p></details>
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>How many adults can accompany the kids?</summary><p>Parents are welcome to stay. Capacity limits apply to children; accompanying adults don't count toward the 16-child max.</p></details>
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>What's included for supervision?</summary><p>Each party has a dedicated Blue Ridge Boost instructor who leads the activity and supervises children throughout the session.</p></details>
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>Is the space accessible?</summary><p>Yes — our Charlottesville location is on one level with step-free access from the parking area.</p></details>
<details class="birthday-faq__item"><summary>What's the deposit and cancellation policy?</summary><p>A deposit is collected to hold your date and is credited toward your total. The remaining balance is due the day of the party. For current deposit amount and reschedule/cancellation terms, please contact us at <a href="mailto:birthdays@blueridgeboost.com">birthdays@blueridgeboost.com</a>.</p></details>
