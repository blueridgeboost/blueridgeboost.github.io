<div class="scholarship-intro">
<p>At Blue Ridge Boost, we are committed to making our classes affordable and encouraging the participation of underrepresented groups in the field of computer science. While we strive to provide a high-quality educational experience at the lowest price, we are aware that our classes and camps are too expensive for some of the children interested in computing.</p>
<p>For that reason, we created three scholarship funds in remembrance of dear friends, three siblings that, unconditionally, loved each other and positively impacted everyone they came in contact with.</p>
</div>
