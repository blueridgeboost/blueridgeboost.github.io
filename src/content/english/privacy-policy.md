<h3>Your Privacy Matters</h3>
<p>Blue Ridge Boost LLC is committed to protecting the privacy of the children enrolled in our classes and their parents/guardians. By enrolling your child in our classes, you consent to the practices described in this policy.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Information Collection</h2>
<p>To ensure the safety and security of the children in our care, we use Ring video cameras to monitor and record activity in our classrooms during class sessions. The video footage may capture the name, likeness, image, voice, and actions of children, staff, and visitors present in the classroom.</p>
<p>The personal information we collect may include:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>Child's name, date of birth, and photograph</li>
<li>Parent/guardian's name, address, email address, and phone number</li>
<li>Emergency contact information</li>
<li>Relevant medical information (allergies, medical conditions, etc.)</li>
<li>Attendance records</li>
<li>Video footage from classroom monitoring</li>
</ul>
<p>We only collect personal information necessary to provide our services, ensure child safety, and comply with legal requirements. We do not collect any personal information directly from children.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Use of Information</h2>
<p>The personal information and video footage collected is used for the following purposes:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>To monitor the safety and well-being of children in our care</li>
<li>To maintain security in our facilities</li>
<li>To provide updates and communicate with parents/guardians</li>
<li>To aid in the training and professional development of our staff</li>
<li>To support the administration of our services</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not use personal information or video footage for any other purposes without parental consent, unless required by law.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Disclosure of Information</h2>
<p>Blue Ridge Boost maintains strict confidentiality of the personal information collected. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer personal information to outside parties. However, we may disclose information in the following circumstances:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>With parental consent</li>
<li>To comply with a court order or other legal process</li>
<li>To protect the rights, property, or safety of Blue Ridge Boost, our staff, students, or others</li>
<li>To address emergencies impacting the health and safety of a child</li>
<li>To outside service providers who need access to fulfill responsibilities on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="policy-section policy-highlight">
<h2>Data Security</h2>
<p>We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Ring video footage is securely stored and encrypted. Access is limited to authorized staff and footage is automatically deleted after a set retention period.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Your Rights and Choices</h2>
<p>As a parent/guardian, you have the right to:</p>
<ul class="policy-list">
<li>Request access to your child's information</li>
<li>Request corrections to inaccurate information</li>
<li>Withdraw consent for the collection of personal information (subject to legal requirements)</li>
</ul>
<p>If you have any questions about exercising these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.</p>
</div>

<div class="policy-section">
<h2>Changes to this Policy</h2>
<p>Blue Ridge Boost reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify parents of any changes that significantly impact the handling of personal information.</p>
