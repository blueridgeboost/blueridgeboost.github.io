How Basic Membership Works

  1. Choose your hours. Four or eight hours per student each month.
  2. Reserve your sessions. Book two-hour blocks within published lab hours.
  3. Bring your adult coach. Under basic membership, your coach supervises the children and leads the team.
  4. Build and explore. Bring your own equipment or purchase equipment access and instructional support.

Lab Policies

Reservations & Monthly Hours

Advance reservations are required. Sessions are booked in two-hour blocks within published lab hours.

Membership requires a minimum purchase of four hours per student each month. Hours cannot be shared or transferred and expire at the end of the membership month. If BRB cancels a session, affected students receive make-up credit.

Adult Coach & Child Supervision

Basic membership does not include BRB-provided child supervision or childcare — basic membership is not a drop-off program.

Every student or team on basic membership must have a designated adult coach present throughout the session. That adult is responsible for supervising the children, managing behavior, and ensuring they follow lab rules.

BRB employees assist with equipment and enforce equipment-use requirements. They are not the children's supervising adults under basic membership.

Students may not remain in the lab without their designated adult coach. If the coach leaves, the students must leave with them unless a replacement supervising adult has been arranged and confirmed.

Private and one-on-one BRB coaching sessions include supervision by the booked BRB coach during the confirmed coaching time. Supervision ends when the booked session ends; outside those windows, basic-membership rules apply.

Equipment Care

Equipment damaged through normal, responsible use will be repaired or replaced at no additional charge. Damage caused by misuse or abuse may result in a repair or replacement charge. Please report equipment problems promptly.

Safe Equipment Use

3D printer use requires safety orientation and staff approval. BRB employees may restrict equipment use or stop unsafe activity. Equipment assistance does not replace the adult coach's responsibility to supervise the children.

Cancellation and no-show terms follow our standard payment policies. Please give as much advance notice as possible when changing a reservation; late cancellations may consume booked hours.
