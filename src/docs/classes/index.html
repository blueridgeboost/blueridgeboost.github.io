Subject Areas

🧮

Math

From enrichment to competition prep (Math Kangaroo, Math Olympiad, AMC 8/10), our math classes build deep understanding, creative problem-solving, and confidence. Small groups of up to 4 students.

💻

Coding

Learn to think like a programmer—from block-based Minecraft coding to Python, C++, and Lua. Algorithmic thinking is at the core of every class.

🤖

Robotics

Build and program real robots! VEX Battle Bots, FIRST LEGO League, and Carnegie Mellon certification courses. Mechanics, electronics, and automation through hands-on projects.

🎮

Game Development

Create your own games! Younger students start with Roblox Studio and Minecraft mods. Teens master 3D modeling in Blender and build assets for Unreal Engine.
