Eligibility notes
  • WRO: Age is based on the age the student turns during the competition calendar year. U.S. event offerings and rules may differ.
  • VEX IQ: Grade and age ranges above are general guidance. Official division eligibility uses the current season's birthdate cutoffs.
  • FIRST K–8 Robotics: Specific offerings and age divisions will be added when details become available over the summer.
  • RoboCupJunior: International eligibility is based on age on July 1 of the competition year. There is no current national U.S. organizing body; U.S. teams typically qualify through the RoboCup Americas Super Regional (hosted in Canada — the closest regional qualifier for U.S. teams), with the global pathway managed via junior.robocup.org. Age minimums can be lower at qualifying events; check each organizer's rules for younger participants.

Equipment suitability depends on the competition, category, and current rules.

Lab Policies

Reservations & Supervision

Advance reservations are required. Staff supervision is included, but instructional coaching is provided only through a coaching add-on. Teams bringing their own coach are led by that coach.

Equipment Care

Equipment damaged through normal, responsible use will be repaired or replaced at no additional charge. Damage caused by misuse or abuse may result in a repair or replacement charge. Please report equipment problems promptly.

Safe Tool Use

3D printer use requires safety orientation and staff approval.
