2026 Theme: Robots Meet Culture

Teams will explore how robots can help shape, protect, and grow art and culture in today's world. From ancient cave paintings to modern digital art, robots are becoming creative partners and protectors of our cultural history.

Competition Categories

🤖

RoboMission

Program autonomous robots to complete missions on a themed playing field. Available at Elementary, Junior, and Senior levels.

🏎️

Future Engineers

Build a self-driving vehicle that navigates a track autonomously. Focuses on sensors, AI, and precision control.

Team Eligibility

Requirements to Participate

  • Register directly through Blue Ridge Boost (no separate pass required)
  • Meet WRO age and category eligibility requirements
  • Teams of 2-3 members plus one coach

Teams cannot compete unless their coach has completed the background check.
