Frequently Asked

Can we bring our own food or cake?

Yes — you're welcome to bring your own cake, food, and drinks with any package. Please let us know about any allergies in your guest list so we can accommodate.

How early can we arrive to set up?

Setup access is included in your booked time slot. If you need extra setup time, mention it when you request the party and we'll see what we can do.

How many adults can accompany the kids?

Parents are welcome to stay. Capacity limits apply to children; accompanying adults don't count toward the 16-child max.

What's included for supervision?

Each party has a dedicated Blue Ridge Boost instructor who leads the activity and supervises children throughout the session.

Is the space accessible?

Yes — our Charlottesville location is on one level with step-free access from the parking area.

What's the deposit and cancellation policy?

A deposit is collected to hold your date and is credited toward your total. The remaining balance is due the day of the party. For current deposit amount and reschedule/cancellation terms, please contact us at birthdays@blueridgeboost.com.
