WRO Robotics Lab

Equipment included. Help when you need it.

$129 per team / month · Two two-hour sessions

Join the WRO Robotics Lab

Build, program, and practice together using our robotics equipment. An intermediate option for teams that can work independently but benefit from occasional guidance.

Included with your membership

  • A dedicated LEGO Education SPIKE Prime or VEX IQ set for use during lab sessions
  • A tablet or Chromebook for programming
  • Access to our practice tables
  • Shared assistance with setup, building, programming, and basic troubleshooting

Teams work primarily independently. Dedicated coaching is available separately for structured instruction, extensive troubleshooting, or focused competition preparation.

Session times

10:00 AM – 12:00 PM or 4:00 – 6:00 PM

Reserve two sessions each month during designated staffed lab hours. Advance reservations and an adult team supervisor are required.

Equipment care

  • Equipment remains at Blue Ridge Boost.
  • Equipment that breaks under normal, responsible use will be repaired or replaced at no additional charge.
  • Teams will be charged for repair or replacement costs resulting from equipment misuse or abuse.
  • Please report damaged or malfunctioning equipment promptly.

Equipment type is confirmed at enrollment, subject to availability. Competition registration is separate; equipment suitability depends on the applicable WRO category and current rules.
